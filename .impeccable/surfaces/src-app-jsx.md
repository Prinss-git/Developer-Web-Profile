# Portfolio home (single page)

Scope: the whole one-page portfolio (src/App.jsx and its sections) plus the 404 page. Visitor mode: Experience, with a Persuade close (resume, message, live demo).

Audience: recruiters (10-second scan), engineers (click demos), local clients. Action: open a live project, download resume, send a message. Constraint: light/dark toggle stays.

Memorable moment: MotoTrack as entry No. 001, stamped LIVE IN PRODUCTION, with the stamp landing as the record is "certified".

STORY: Visitor sees a record of a real person, reads entry 001 is running a real shop, opens it; scans further entries, timeline, skills as "subjects"; requests a copy (resume) or files a request (contact form).
