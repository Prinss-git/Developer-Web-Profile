# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Three audiences, weighted equally:
- Internship recruiters and HR screening many student candidates quickly; they need to see "this person ships real software" within seconds.
- Developer leads and engineers who click the live demos, read stack details, and judge technical depth.
- Prospective freelance clients (local businesses, like the motor parts shop behind MotoTrack) looking for someone to build them a working system.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio for Prince Christian Parnada, a 3rd-year BSIT student at the University of Cebu – Banilad (Cebu, Philippines). It exists to land an internship or entry-level role starting 2026, and to open freelance work. Success: a visitor opens a live project, downloads the resume, or sends a message.

## Positioning
A student whose flagship project is in real production: MotoTrack runs inventory, sales, and forecasting for an actual single-store motor parts business in Cebu (2,000+ SKUs, 158 categories). Real users, not classroom demos.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, Facebook/Viber shares, resumes, and job applications, often on phones. They evaluate by clicking live demos (MotoTrack offers demo credentials) and GitHub.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- React 19 + Vite + Tailwind 3, deployed on Vercel at prince-parnada.vercel.app.
- Sections: Hero, About, Projects, Experience & Skills, CTA/Contact, Footer; 404 page.
- Contact form via EmailJS (env vars VITE_EMAILJS_*).
- Light/dark theme toggle must be preserved (user-binding).
- Resume linked at /resume.pdf.
- Content lives in src/data (projects, experience, skills).

## Product Principles
1. Proof over claims: lead with shipped, live work.
2. Respect a recruiter's 10 seconds and an engineer's 10 minutes on the same page.
3. Honest scale: a strong student, never an inflated "agency".
4. Every project is one click from running.
